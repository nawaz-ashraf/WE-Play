Effective date: 2026-04-04

This Privacy Policy explains how WE Play ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses,
shares, and protects information when you use the WE Play mobile application
(the "App"). By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1) INFORMATION WE COLLECT

A) Information you provide
- Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email
  address, and profile photo as provided by Google.
- Support requests: if you contact support, we collect the information you
  choose to share, such as your email address and message content.

B) Information collected automatically
- Device and app information: device model, OS version, language, time zone,
  app version, and crash diagnostics.
- Usage data: gameplay events, session duration, screens viewed, and in-app
  interactions.
- Network data: IP address and general location derived from IP.

C) Advertising data
- Our advertising partners (for example, Google AdMob) may collect device
  identifiers, ad interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve and

  interactions with ads.

We use the information to:
- Provide and maintain the App and its features.
- Sync progress across devices when you sign in.
- Display ads and measure ad performance.
- Improve app stability, performance, and user experience.
- Respond to support requests and communicate with you.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

3) SHARING OF INFORMATION

We may share information with:
- Service providers that help us operate the App (for example, analytics,

We do not sell your personal information.

The App uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, update, or delete
personal information. You can:

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
