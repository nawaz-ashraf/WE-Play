# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-04-04

This Privacy Policy explains how Miniplay Hub ("we", "us") collects, uses, and
shares information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

- Account information: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email,
  and profile photo as provided by Google.
- App data: gameplay progress, settings, and other data you choose to save.
- Device information: device model, OS version, language, and IP address.
- Advertising data: our ad partners (for example, Google AdMob) may collect
  device identifiers and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads.

## How We Use Information

- Provide and maintain the app.
- Sync progress across devices.
- Display ads and measure ad performance.
- Improve app stability and user experience.

## Third-Party Services

We use the following services, which may collect information in accordance with
their own privacy policies:

- Google Sign-In
- Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage
- Google AdMob

## Data Retention

We retain information as long as needed to provide the service or comply with
legal obligations. You can request deletion of your account data.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has
provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Your Choices

- You can sign out of Google at any time.
- You can disable ad personalization in your device settings.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date
at the top of this page when changes are made.
